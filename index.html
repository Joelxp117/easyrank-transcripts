💬 Support & FAQ

We're here to help

Got a question? Our team is available 24/7 on Discord — usually replies within minutes.

How fast is the boost? +
Our boosters start on your order within hours of payment confirmation. The exact time depends on the rank range — typically between 1 and 7 days. Higher ranks take longer.
Is my account safe? +
Yes. Every booster is verified and has been personally vetted by our team. We use secure communication for credentials and never share your account with third parties. All orders are fully insured.
How do I redeem my code? +
After payment you'll receive a 10-character redemption code. Join our Discord, open the ticket panel, select "Redeem Code", paste your code, and a private ticket will be created for you.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We currently accept Litecoin (LTC). Payments are verified automatically on the blockchain, so you get your delivery code within seconds of sending. More payment methods coming soon.
What if I paid but got no code? +
If your payment was sent correctly but no code appeared, open a support ticket on Discord with your TX hash. Our team will resolve it within minutes.
Can I get a refund? +
Refunds are handled case-by-case. If the boost hasn't started yet, contact us within 1 hour of purchase. Once a booster has started, partial refunds may be issued depending on progress.
